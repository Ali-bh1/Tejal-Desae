Business Expansion Code is for founders scaling, running the 8 Pillars: delegation, pricing at scale, visibility, team, decisions. Not sure which? No. This is mentoring and coaching. It's not a replacement for medical or psychological care, and if you're working with a therapist, please keep going — this sits alongside that work, never instead of it.
