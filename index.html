A Letter from
Tejal

I spent 25 years inside some of the world's most demanding global organisations. Consulting. Senior leadership. The rooms where decisions with nine zeros attached were made.

I was called when everything needed holding together. Reliable. Capable. The woman who delivered.

And in the silence between boardroom decisions and 4am sadhana — I was also a Das Mahavidya initiate. A Shiv sadhak. A woman who had sat with the darkest goddesses in the Hindu pantheon and come back changed.

Two marriages. Carrying cycles of someone else's debt. A son I raised entirely alone through five years of fever and fear. A spiritual practice that held me when nothing else could.

I did not come to this work theoretically. I came to it through everything.

What eventually became clear is this: no amount of mindset work, strategy, or spiritual reframing could soften what was happening beneath the surface. Because it wasn't a thought problem — it was a safety pattern.

My body didn't know how to receive without staying vigilant, high-functioning, and hyper focused. Rest felt conditional. Support felt unfamiliar. Ease felt like something that had to be justified.

Once I stopped trying to fix myself and started listening instead, everything changed — gently, steadily, without force. I learned that these patterns weren't flaws. They were intelligent adaptations.

Today I work at Stage 3 of manifestation. Not vision boards. Not positive thinking. Stage 3 is where the body becomes the instrument. Where the ancestral field is cleared — not just understood. Where the quantum field receives a completely different signal.

If something in you just exhaled — that is the whale frequency. That is recognition. You are already home.

With love and gratitude
Tejal Desae
