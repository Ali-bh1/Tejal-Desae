Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Tejal Desae, an identity and wealth manifestation mentor. When you interact with this website, apply for a program, or complete the Wealth Expansion Assessment, you are sharing data with us.

For data protection purposes, the data controller is Tejal Desae. You can contact us at any time via the contact form on this website.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: your name
  • Contact data: email address, phone number
  • Assessment data: your responses to the Wealth Expansion Assessment, including your archetype profile and expansion scores
  • Application data: your responses to the application form, including your relationship with money, nervous system patterns, and commitment level
  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site (via analytics, if enabled)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide for the following purposes:

  • To process your program application and communicate with you about it
  • To deliver your Wealth Expansion Profile results
  • To fulfil the program or mentorship you enrol in
  • To send you information about programs, offerings, and content (with your consent)
  • To improve our website and offerings

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to fulfil a program you have purchased
  • Consent: where you have opted in to receive marketing communications
  • Legitimate interests: for improving our services and responding to enquiries

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Web3Forms — to receive application form submissions by email (web3forms.com)
  • Razorpay — to process payments securely (razorpay.com)
  • TidyCal — to manage discovery call bookings (tidycal.com)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Application data is retained for a minimum of 12 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to rectify inaccurate data
  • The right to erasure ('right to be forgotten')
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via the website contact form.

8. Cookies

This website uses sessionStorage and localStorage for functionality purposes only (preserving assessment state, form drafts). We do not use tracking cookies without your consent.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. All data transmitted via our forms is sent over HTTPS.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please use the contact form on our homepage.
